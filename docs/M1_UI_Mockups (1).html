Delete my account
Permanently removes your data after a 30-day grace period
Regulatory mapping
  • Granular consent toggles — GDPR Art. 7(3): withdrawing consent must be as easy as giving it. Each purpose is separate.
  • Request a copy satisfies GDPR Art. 15/20 (access & portability) and CCPA “right to know”. Machine-readable JSON + CSV.
  • “Never sold or shared” stated in plain sight — supports the CCPA/CPRA position that no opt-out of sale is required.
  • Deletion entry point lives here, next to the policies, so rights are discoverable in one place.
States
  • Export links are time-limited and expire automatically — a stale export is a standing data-breach risk.
  • Toggle changes write a timestamped ConsentRecord; consent history is retained as evidence.

Delete your account

This removes your account and everything in it.

⚠️What gets deleted Your profile, all imported trades, plans, strategies, journal entries, checklists and AI reviews. Broker connections are revoked immediately.

Before you go

You may want a copy of your trading history first — it can’t be recovered afterwards.

30-day grace period

Your account is locked immediately and permanently erased after 30 days. Sign in during that window to cancel. After that it cannot be undone.

🗓️

Deletion scheduled

Your account will be permanently erased on 22 August 2026. Until then you can undo this at any time.

🔌Broker connections revokedTrade syncing has stopped. Reconnecting requires cancelling the deletion first.

A confirmation has been emailed to raj@example.com.

Why the friction is intentional
  • Type-to-confirm prevents an accidental irreversible action; the destructive button stays disabled until the word matches exactly.
  • Export offered inline — the user is most likely to want their data at exactly this moment.
  • Immediate broker revocation — credentials for a departing user are a liability, so they are cut at request time, not after the grace period.
  • 30-day grace balances GDPR erasure (“without undue delay”) against protecting users from mistakes and rage-quits.
What survives deletion
  • Financial and tax records required by law are retained in a separate ledger, unlinked from the deleted identity.
  • Anonymised, non-identifying aggregates may remain — this is stated in the privacy policy rather than hidden.
  • Backups age out on their own cycle; the retention schedule in the spec sets the maximum window.
