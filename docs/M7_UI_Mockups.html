Ask the coach

A read-only Q&A surface for a trader to ask about their own history in plain language — every answer cites the trades it's grounded in, and the coach says so when it doesn't have enough evidence (Figure 7.2).

Ask the coach

Answers are grounded in your own trades — nothing from anyone else.

💬Ask me anything about your trading history. I can only see your own data — nothing from other traders, and I can't place or edit trades.
Why did I lose money last week?
Two trades account for most of it: a GOLD short on Tuesday (-$310) and an ES long on Thursday (-$240) — both were sized larger than your typical trade and both broke the stop-loss rule from your Scaled ES opening range checklist.
GOLD · Tue · -$310ES · Thu · -$240
Is that a pattern?
Not yet — that's only 2 trades, which isn't enough to call it a pattern. I'll keep watching; if it happens a few more times I'll raise it as an insight on your feed.
Elements
  • Evidence chip under a coach message — the same underlying concept as an insight's evidence trail (M7-S02), scoped to that one answer instead of a standing insight.
  • Starter prompts are suggestions only; a trader can type anything, but off-topic or non-trading questions get a plain "I can only help with your trading history" response.
Rules
  • The coach never answers with a claim it can't ground in the trader's own M3/M4/M5 data — if it can't find a grounded answer, it says so rather than speculating (Section on AI grounding).
  • Chat is read-only end to end: it never creates or edits a trade, strategy, or journal entry, regardless of how the request is phrased.
