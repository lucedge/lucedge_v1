# **DecisionOS — UI/UX Design System**

## **Product Vision**

DecisionOS is **not a trading platform**.

It is **not a broker dashboard**.

It is **not an analytics terminal**.

It is **not accounting software.**

DecisionOS is a **personal operating system for traders**.

Its purpose is to help traders consistently make better trading decisions through planning, execution, review, and behavioural improvement.

The UI should continuously reinforce one idea:

> **Become a better trader, not just a more profitable one.**

Everything in the interface should encourage calm, discipline, consistency and progress instead of excitement, urgency or gambling.

---

# **Core Emotional Principles**

The UI should make users feel:

* Calm  
* Focused  
* In control  
* Objective  
* Professional  
* Improving every day

The UI should never feel like:

* Gambling  
* Watching live markets  
* Chasing profits  
* Competing with others  
* Hyperactive trading

Think:

> Apple Health

> Notion

> Arc Browser

> Linear

> Headspace

Instead of

> TradingView

> Binance

> Bloomberg Terminal

> Broker dashboards

---

# **Design Philosophy**

The application is built around one concept:

## **Progress \> Profit**

The application should reward

* consistency  
* discipline  
* following rules  
* completing reviews  
* respecting risk

—not making money.

A losing trade can still be displayed as an excellent decision.

A profitable trade can still be displayed as a poor decision.

Decision quality becomes the visual centre of the application.

---

# **Overall Visual Style**

## **General Feel**

Minimal.

Quiet.

Confident.

Modern.

Premium.

Professional.

Every screen should contain generous whitespace.

The interface should breathe.

Information density should remain intentionally low.

Avoid overwhelming the trader with dozens of widgets.

One important insight is always better than twenty charts.

---

# **Colour Philosophy**

The application should never psychologically train traders to associate green with "good" and red with "bad".

Instead:

Colours represent process quality.

### **Success Colour**

Represents

* followed plan  
* disciplined execution  
* review completed  
* progress

NOT profit.

---

### **Warning Colour**

Represents

Needs review.

Incomplete.

Potential issue.

---

### **Error Colour**

Represents

Rule violations.

Repeated mistakes.

Behavioural issues.

Never losing money.

---

### **Neutral Colours**

Large use of

Greys

Soft backgrounds

Muted cards

Low contrast surfaces

Accent colours should be rare.

Colour should direct attention.

Never decorate.

---

# **Typography**

Large typography.

High readability.

Minimal font weights.

Very little visual noise.

Headers should feel calm.

Examples

Good Morning

Today's Focus

Your Discipline Improved

Review Complete

Not

TRADING PERFORMANCE ANALYTICS

Home

Trade

Review

"What can I improve?"

Progress

"Am I becoming more disciplined?"

Coach

"What should I do next?"

Example hierarchy

Greeting

↓

Today's Mission

Discipline Score

↓

Current Streak

# **Trading Session Mode**

One of the product's signature experiences.

When the trader presses

"Start Trading Session"

The application enters Focus Mode.

Everything unrelated disappears.

Visible information

Today's trading plan

Maximum risk

Trades remaining

Current setup checklist

Current discipline

Time remaining in session

No analytics.

No historical charts.

No distractions.

This becomes the trader's workspace.

---

# **Navigation Philosophy**

Maximum five primary tabs.

Home

Trades

Review

Progress

Coach

Everything else lives inside these sections.

Avoid menu explosion.

---

# **Component Philosophy**

Cards over tables.

Timeline over spreadsheets.

Visual summaries over data grids.

Progress rings over percentage labels.

Checklists over forms.

Large touch targets.

Minimal scrolling.

---

Never administrative.

Workflow

↓

Quick Reflection

20–30 seconds.

# **Forms**

Prefer

Selection

Toggle

Buttons

Sliders

Tags

Radio buttons

Multi-select chips

Setup quality

Execution

Review completion

Not reporting.

Examples

Discipline

84%

Consistency

Level 12

Rule Adherence

---

# **Gamification**

Gamification exists to build habits.

Not addiction.

XP is awarded for

Logging trades

Completing reviews

Following rules

Preparing before market

Weekly reviews

Maintaining discipline

Skipping poor setups

Not for

Winning trades

Higher PnL

Larger profits

More trades

Never reward gambling behaviour.

---

# **Streak System**

Streaks should measure

Habits.

Examples

Daily Journal Streak

Weekly Review Streak

Plan Adherence Streak

Risk Discipline Streak

Never

Winning streak.

---

# **Badge System**

Badges represent behavioural achievements.

Examples

30 Days

Without Moving Stop Loss

Completed 25 Reviews

100 Rule-Compliant Trades

Perfect Discipline Week

Accepted 20 Planned Losses

These reinforce identity rather than performance.

---

# **Analytics Philosophy**

Analytics should answer

"What should I change?"

Not

"What happened?"

Instead of

Win Rate

63%

Prefer

Morning trades outperform afternoon trades by 38%.

Trade only before lunch next week.

Every metric should produce an action.

---

# **Charts**

Charts should be secondary.

Never dominate the interface.

Preferred charts

Equity curve

Discipline trend

Decision Score trend

Rule adherence trend

Setup comparison

Session comparison

Avoid decorative charts.

---

# **AI Coach**

The AI should behave like

A mentor.

Not ChatGPT.

Tone

Objective.

Professional.

Evidence-based.

Calm.

Never motivational.

Never speculative.

Never predicts markets.

It explains

your behaviour.

Example

Yesterday

You violated your sizing rule twice.

Those trades accounted for 72% of yesterday's losses.

Would you like to review them?

---

# **Behaviour Engine**

Behaviour should appear naturally throughout the UI.

Instead of

"You traded emotionally."

Display

Detected Pattern

Revenge Trading

Observed 3 times

Concrete.

Objective.

Actionable.

---

# **Notifications**

Notifications should encourage process.

Examples

Review today's trades.

You have maintained discipline for seven sessions.

Today's plan has not been created.

Never

"Market is moving\!"

or

"You missed a trade\!"

---

# **Motion Design**

Animations should feel deliberate.

Slow.

Elegant.

Purposeful.

Examples

Smooth progress ring updates.

Checklist completion animations.

Decision Score reveal.

Badge unlock.

Avoid flashy trading animations.

---

# **Empty States**

Every empty screen should educate.

Example

No trades reviewed yet.

Reviewing trades consistently improves decision quality.

Import today's trades to begin.

---

# **Microinteractions**

Checklist tick

Soft success animation

XP increase

Decision Score counting upward

Progress ring filling

Subtle haptic feedback (mobile)

Nothing flashy.

---

# **Information Hierarchy**

Every screen follows

Intent

↓

Current Progress

↓

Most Important Action

↓

Supporting Information

↓

Historical Details

Never reverse this hierarchy.

---

# **Mobile First Philosophy**

The product should work exceptionally well on mobile.

Trade review should take

under one minute.

Desktop adds

larger dashboards

comparisons

analytics

multi-session review

but mobile should support the complete daily workflow.

---

# **Accessibility**

High contrast.

Readable typography.

Large touch targets.

Keyboard shortcuts on desktop.

Minimal reliance on colour alone.

Every status should have text and iconography.

---

# **Product Identity**

DecisionOS should not feel like software for analysing trades.

It should feel like software for becoming a better trader.

The interface should consistently reinforce these principles:

* Decisions over outcomes  
* Discipline over dopamine  
* Progress over profit  
* Clarity over complexity  
* Consistency over intensity  
* Reflection over reaction

---

# **Final Design North Star**

If a trader opens the application every morning, the UI should make them feel like a professional athlete opening a training journal—not a gambler opening a brokerage app.

Every interaction should quietly answer one question:

> **"Is this helping the trader make better decisions?"**

If the answer is no, it doesn't belong in the interface.
